Per level: L0 14, L1 12, L2 44, L3 29, L4 28, L5 53, L6 37, L7 47.
Only L2 was materially wasteful (~6 actions lost to a bookkeeping slip of mine,

## The game

Board 64x64 = a 21x21 grid of 3x3 CELLS (rows/cols 0..62). Row 63 is a static 5
bar. Col 63 is a move counter: one pixel per action, top-down, reset each level;
colour 5 while the only axis is vertical, else c. Background 9.
Classify a cell by its CORNER pixel — the CENTRE pixel carries markers.

Objects:
- PLAYERS: hollow 5 shapes, rigid, translate only. Components are 8-CONNECTED
  (level 5's four diagonally-touching shapes move as one piece). They may
  overlap each other (level 3 requires it).
- AXES: full cell-lines of colour a, horizontal and/or vertical, themselves
  movable (immovable and drawn solid only in level 0).
- MIRRORS: every image of every player under the reflection group generated by
  the axes (1 axis -> 1 image, 2 perpendicular axes -> 3, including the
  180-degree rotation about their crossing). Solid 4, clipped per-cell.
- TARGET: static solid b cells.

GOAL: every target cell covered by a player cell or a mirror cell (subset, not
equality — level 4's solution covers 10 cells outside the target).

Actions: 1=up 2=down 3=left 4=right (one cell), 5=cycle the active object,
6=click (never needed), 7=NEVER PROBED (risk it was RESET).
The active object shows 0 in its cell centres. Centre-pixel precedence:
target match marker b > active-axis hole > own-object hole > background.

The action-5 cycle order is NOT derivable from a single frame (level 2 cycles
its players largest-first, levels 5 and 6 smallest-first), so observed orders are
recorded in world_model.CYCLE_ORDER keyed by (n_players, n_axes, sizes).
A wrong guess is free: the misprediction lands on the toggle itself.

## Method that worked

observe -> world_model._scan -> solve.py (axes onto the target's symmetry lines,
then branch-and-bound exact/covering placement) -> simulate the whole plan
through predict() and require level_up on the last step -> commit.
Always re-derive from the actual latest frame; never hand-compute a residual.
